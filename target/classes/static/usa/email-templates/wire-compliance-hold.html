Why your wire is on hold

Our automated AML and treasury controls flagged this instruction for enhanced review. This is a standard safeguard and does not necessarily indicate a problem with your account or the beneficiary. Common reasons include:

  • AML screening — Outbound wires are screened under Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and sanctions (OFAC) policies before release.
  • Volume or pattern review — Sudden spikes in wire activity, structuring-like patterns, or rapid repetitive transfers compared with your typical profile may require manual clearance.
  • International / SWIFT due diligence — Cross-border payments may need additional beneficiary and corridor verification.
  • Savings withdrawal limits — If the funding account is a savings vault, Regulation D–style limits (e.g., six outgoing transfers per calendar month) may apply. Additional activity in the same month can trigger a hold until the next monthly cycle.
  • High-value threshold — Transfers that materially exceed your recent wire history may be routed to treasury for approval.
